Frequently Answered Questions

How does 16x acceleration differ from typical ad blocking?

Traditional adblockers sever network connections to ad servers. On platforms like Hotstar and Prime Video that stitch ad segments directly into encrypted HLS/DASH media manifests, severing connections causes infinite buffering loops or media decoder errors. HyperSkip allows the stream to progress normally while multiplying HTML5 playback velocity by 16x and muting audio, completing a 30-second ad break in ~1.8 seconds.

Does this interfere with DRM or violate encryption standards?

No. HyperSkip does not decrypt, alter, or intercept Widevine, PlayReady, or FairPlay data. It communicates exclusively with standard HTML5 Media Element properties (playbackRate, muted) in a secure, sandboxed context script.

Why does screen-sharing in Google Meet or Microsoft Teams produce black video?

Commercial streaming services enforce HDCP and OS-level secure video surfaces. The operating system GPU driver intentionally masks protected video planes from virtual capture buffers (Meet, Teams, Zoom). HyperSkip includes a Presenter Mode that suppresses on-screen indicators and HUD badges during work calls.
