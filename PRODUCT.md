# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Viewers of OTT streaming platforms (JioHotstar, JioCinema, Amazon Prime Video) on Chromium browsers who want seamless, uninterrupted playback without commercial disruptions.

## Product Purpose

HyperSkip is a zero-freeze streaming playback automation tool. It automatically accelerates in-video commercial interruptions to 16x speed, attenuates commercial audio spikes, and triggers skip cues with zero DRM interference.

## Positioning

Unlike traditional ad blockers that break encrypted HLS/DASH streaming chunks and freeze media players, HyperSkip operates within userland HTML5 media parameters, finishing 30-second commercial breaks in under 2 seconds.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities: 16x fast-forward, instant audio mute/restore, skip button auto-dispatch, intro/recap skipping, telemetry tracking.
- Constraints: Manifest V3 compliant. Zero monkey-patching of DRM license exchanges (Widevine/PlayReady).
